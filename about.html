COMPANY PROFILE

Royal Steel Mills Limited stands as a beacon of manufacturing excellence in Kenya’s steel industry. From our state-of-the-art facility in Nakuru, we produce premium TMT bars that form the backbone of Kenya’s growing infrastructure.


With unwavering commitment to quality, innovative production techniques, and a deep understanding of construction needs across the Rift Valley and beyond, we’ve established ourselves as the preferred partner for contractors, developers, and builders who refuse to compromise on structural integrity.

Our Mission

Royal Steel Mills Limited is dedicated to transforming Kenya's construction landscape by manufacturing premium TMT bars that exceed industry standards.

Our Vision

We aspire to be Kenya's undisputed leader in steel manufacturing, recognized for setting new benchmarks in quality, reliability, and customer satisfaction.

Our Core Values

At the core of Royal Steel Mills are the principles of uncompromising quality, unwavering integrity, and continuous innovation.
