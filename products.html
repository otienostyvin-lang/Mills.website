TMT Bar Features Description

Built to Last

Our TMT bars feature a unique composition with a tough outer layer and a ductile core, providing the perfect balance of strength and flexibility. This innovative structure offers enhanced earthquake resistance, superior bendability, and excellent weldability.

With high yield strength and uniform mechanical properties, Royal Steel TMT bars deliver consistent performance in even the most demanding construction environments.
