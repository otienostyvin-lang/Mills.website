WIRE ROD

Product Description

Wire Rod is a premium-quality, hot-rolled steel product supplied in coil form for use in a wide range of construction, manufacturing, and engineering applications. It is produced from high-grade steel billets and offers excellent strength, ductility, and formability. Wire rod serves as the primary raw material for manufacturing binding wire, wire mesh, nails, screws, fencing wire, welding electrodes, springs, fasteners, and many other steel products.

  • Product Type: Hot-Rolled Steel Wire Rod
  • Material: Low-carbon, medium-carbon, high-carbon, or alloy steel
  • Manufacturing Process: Hot rolling of steel billets followed by controlled cooling
  • Diameter Range: Typically 5.5 mm – 25 mm
  • Coil Weight: Commonly 1–3 metric tonnes (custom weights available)
  • Steel Grades: SAE, ASTM, BS, EN, JIS, or equivalent international grades
  • Tensile Strength: Approximately 350–1,200 MPa (depending on steel grade)
  • Surface Finish: Smooth hot-rolled finish, free from major surface defects
  • Standards: Manufactured in accordance with ASTM, BS, EN, ISO, and JIS standards
  • Applications: Manufacturing of binding wire, BRC/Weldmesh, fencing wire, barbed wire, nails, screws, bolts, chains, welding electrodes, springs, wire ropes, fasteners, and other engineering and construction products.

BRC & WELDMESH

Product Description

BRC (British Reinforcement Concrete) Weldmesh is a prefabricated steel reinforcement mesh made from high-strength steel wires that are electrically welded at every intersection to form a uniform grid pattern. It provides superior reinforcement for concrete structures, improving strength, crack resistance, and construction efficiency. BRC Weldmesh is widely used in residential, commercial, industrial, and infrastructure projects.

  • Product Type: Welded Steel Reinforcement Mesh (BRC Weldmesh)
  • Material: High-tensile or mild steel wire
  • Manufacturing Process: Electric resistance welding (ERW) at each wire intersection
  • Diameter Range: Typically 4 mm – 16 mm
  • Mesh Opening Sizes: Commonly 100 × 100 mm, 150 × 150 mm, 200 × 200 mm, or customized sizes
  • Panel Dimensions: Standard 2.4 m × 6.0 m (custom sizes available)
  • Steel Grade: High Yield Strength Steel (typically 500 MPa or as specified)
  • Surface Finish: Black steel or galvanized for enhanced corrosion resistance
  • Standards: Manufactured in accordance with BS 4483, ASTM A1064, ISO, or equivalent international standards
  • Applications: Reinforced concrete slabs, foundations, walls, pavements, roads, bridges, retaining walls, drainage structures, warehouses, industrial floors, and precast concrete products.

BINDING WIRE

Product Description

Binding wire is a soft, annealed steel wire designed for securely tying reinforcing bars (rebar), wire mesh, and other construction materials. Its high flexibility, durability, and ease of twisting make it an essential product for construction, civil engineering, agriculture, and industrial applications. It provides a firm and reliable hold while remaining easy to handle on-site.

  • Product Type: Annealed Steel Binding Wire
  • Material: Low-carbon steel
  • Manufacturing Process: Wire drawing followed by annealing for increased flexibility
  • Wire Diameter: Typically 0.8 mm – 2.0 mm (common sizes: 1.2 mm, 1.4 mm, 1.6 mm)
  • Tensile Strength: Approximately 300–450 MPa (depending on grade)
  • Surface Finish: Black annealed, galvanized, or PVC-coated
  • Coil Weight: Commonly 5 kg, 10 kg, 25 kg, 50 kg, or customized
  • Elongation: High ductility for easy twisting without breaking
  • Standards: Manufactured in accordance with ASTM, BS, EN, ISO, or equivalent international standards
  • Applications: Tying reinforcing bars (rebar), securing BRC/Weldmesh, construction formwork, fencing, packaging, agriculture, and general industrial fastening.

NAILS

Product Description

Steel nails are durable fastening products manufactured from high-quality steel wire for use in construction, woodworking, roofing, packaging, and general industrial applications. Designed for strength, reliability, and easy installation, they provide secure fastening in timber, concrete (specialized types), masonry, and other building materials. Steel nails are available in a wide range of sizes and finishes to meet different project requirements.

  • Material: Low-carbon or high-carbon steel
  • Manufacturing Process: Cold-formed from steel wire with precision-cut and hardened points
  • Length Range: Typically 20 mm – 200 mm
  • Diameter Range: Typically 1.2 mm – 6.0 mm
  • Head Type: Flat head, round head, lost head, or umbrella head (depending on application)
  • Point Type: Diamond point or sharp point for easy penetration
  • Surface Finish: Bright, galvanized, electro-galvanized, hot-dip galvanized, or polished
  • Tensile Strength: High strength for secure and long-lasting fastening
  • Standards: Manufactured in accordance with ASTM, BS, EN, ISO, or equivalent international standards
  • Applications: Timber construction, furniture manufacturing, roofing, pallets, packaging, fencing, formwork, carpentry, masonry (concrete nails), and general building works.

HOLLOW SECTION

Product Description

Hollow Sections are high-strength structural steel tubes manufactured in square, rectangular, or circular profiles. They are widely used in construction, infrastructure, industrial fabrication, and engineering projects due to their excellent strength-to-weight ratio, durability, and clean appearance. Hollow sections provide superior load-bearing capacity, ease of fabrication, and excellent resistance to bending and torsion, making them ideal for structural frameworks and architectural applications.

  • Product Type: Structural Steel Hollow Section (SHS, RHS & CHS)
  • Material: High-quality mild steel or structural steel
  • Shapes: Square Hollow Section (SHS), Rectangular Hollow Section (RHS), Circular Hollow Section (CHS)
  • Wall Thickness: Typically 1.2 mm – 16 mm
  • Length: Standard 6 m and 12 m (custom lengths available)
  • Manufacturing Process: Cold-formed or hot-finished steel sections with high-frequency welded seams
  • Surface Finish: Black steel, galvanized, or painted
  • Steel Grade: S235, S275, S355, ASTM A500 Grade B/C, or equivalent
  • Standards: Manufactured in accordance with ASTM A500, EN 10219, BS 1387, ISO, or equivalent international standards
  • Applications: Building structures, warehouses, bridges, roofing systems, steel frames, towers, gates, fencing, handrails, machinery, furniture, and general engineering fabrication.

LOUVERS

Product Description

Steel Louvers are durable architectural and ventilation systems designed to allow the free flow of air while protecting buildings from rain, direct sunlight, dust, and debris. They enhance ventilation, improve energy efficiency, and add a modern aesthetic to commercial, industrial, and residential buildings. Steel louvers are available in fixed or adjustable blade designs and can be customized to meet specific architectural and ventilation requirements.

  • Product Type: Steel Architectural Louvers
  • Material: Galvanized steel, mild steel, stainless steel, or aluminum
  • Blade Type: Fixed or adjustable horizontal/vertical blades
  • Blade Thickness: Typically 0.8 mm – 3.0 mm
  • Frame Thickness: Typically 1.2 mm – 3.0 mm
  • Panel Size: Custom-made to project requirements
  • Surface Finish: Powder-coated, galvanized, painted, anodized (for aluminum), or stainless steel finish
  • Ventilation Efficiency: High free-air opening for maximum airflow with minimal pressure loss
  • Weather Resistance: Designed to resist rain penetration, corrosion, and UV exposure
  • Standards: Manufactured in accordance with ASTM, BS, EN, ISO, or equivalent international standards
  • Applications: Building facades, ventilation systems, plant rooms, data centers, warehouses, commercial buildings, industrial facilities, schools, hospitals, and architectural screening.
